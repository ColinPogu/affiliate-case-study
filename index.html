What I built

A full program, not a plugin someone else configured.

The engine

  • Four tiered programs so partners are managed by performance, not lumped together
  • Auto-approval sign-up page and a manual-approval form, plus a Shopify checkout widget that recruits buyers at the moment they purchase
  • Lifecycle email: post-purchase recruitment, sign-up, and timed follow-ups, every one measured
  • Transactional email: referral-sale, payout, and login-nudge notifications

The measurement

  • Per-partner links and codes generated automatically
  • First-party click and order attribution I wrote myself, not a vendor black box
  • ROI, CPA and AOV reporting against a baseline
  • Batched payouts through Tremendous with a fraud-risk check before money moves

Stack: Shopify (store and checkout) · Social Snowball (programs, tiers, sign-up flows, email) · Tremendous (payouts) · GA4 and Search Console · custom attribution.

The standout partner

One relationship, nurtured properly.

A single top partner has driven 229 referrals and about $9,814 in revenue. Finding, onboarding and keeping a creator like that is the difference between a program that plateaus and one that compounds.

What carries over

The transferable part is not the headcount.

It is the instrumentation: segmenting an audience by behavior, building the lifecycle automation that moves them, wiring the attribution so every dollar traces to a source, and reporting ROI and CPA against a baseline instead of a vanity number. That is the same motion whether the unit is an affiliate, a free user converting to a paid team seat, or a trial converting to a subscription.
